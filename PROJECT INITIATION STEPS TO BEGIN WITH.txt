1. **Initialize the Directory:** Create your new project folder and populate your `.agents/` directory with all **eight** configured system instruction files (`planner.md`, `builder.md`, `validator.md`, `context_janitor.md`, `chaos_monkey.md`, `telemetry_sentry.md`, `release_wrangler.md`, `docs_synchronizer.md`).
2. **Open the Antigravity App:** Launch the standalone app and select **File -> Open Workspace** to point it at your newly configured project folder.
3. **Fire the Master Architect Prompt:** Paste the Strategic Initialization Prompt into the blank chat box, filling in your 2-3 sentence project vision at the bottom.
4. **Run Pre-Flight Sweeps:** The system will automatically trigger the **Context Janitor** to spider the repository topology, map existing module signatures, and dump the structural blueprint to `/.plans/context-map.json` so the Planner doesn't operate blind.
5. **Execute the `/grill-me` Interview:** Let the **Planner** run its native `/grill-me` protocol. Answer its cynical, hard-hitting questions about your data structures, dependencies, and environment constraints directly in the chat window.
6. **Survive the Chaos Monkey:** Once the Planner drafts the initial plan, the **Chaos Monkey** will automatically intercept it. Expect it to forcefully modify the document to inject a `## Failure Modes & Mitigation` section addressing edge cases, network drops, and race conditions.
7. **Approve the Blueprint:** Once the final, hardened specification document settles inside your `/.plans/` folder, review its updated layout, verify the immutable data contracts, and type **"Approved"** to hand the execution over to the **Builder**, **Telemetry Sentry**, and **Validator** pipeline.
